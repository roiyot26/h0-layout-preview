The Workshop

Virtual Garden

A Chrome extension that grows a small garden from the time you spend on sites you mark as productive. The rest of your browsing makes it wilt. It’s built with WXT (Manifest V3), TypeScript and Vitest, with CI on every PR, and it has four looks: zen, cosmic, ocean and pixel.
