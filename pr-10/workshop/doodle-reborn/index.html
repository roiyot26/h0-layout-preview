Doodle Reborn

A turn-based draw-and-guess game for two. One player draws a word, and the other watches the drawing replay stroke by stroke, then spells the answer from letter tiles. You play pass-and-play on one phone or async on two, and turns travel as a paper plane across a 3D desk. It’s built with Next.js, React 19, TypeScript and three.js (React Three Fiber), with Neon Postgres on Vercel, and Vitest with CI.
