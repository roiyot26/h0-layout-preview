Virtual Garden

A Chrome extension that grows a small garden from the time you spend on sites you mark as productive. The rest of your browsing makes it wilt. It’s built with WXT (Manifest V3), TypeScript and Vitest, with CI on every PR, and it has four looks: zen, cosmic, ocean and pixel.

chord-cycle-open

A chord wheel you play in the browser with a pointer, touch or your webcam. MediaPipe hand tracking picks the chord, opening your hand swells it, and Web Audio plays it. No plugins and no MIDI hardware.

  • Repo
  • Live demo

Doodle Reborn

A turn-based draw-and-guess game for two. One player draws a word, and the other watches the drawing replay stroke by stroke, then spells the answer from letter tiles. You play pass-and-play on one phone or async on two, and turns travel as a paper plane across a 3D desk. It’s built with Next.js, React 19, TypeScript and three.js (React Three Fiber), with Neon Postgres on Vercel, and Vitest with CI.
